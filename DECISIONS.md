# Decisions — Field Ecologist: Sampling the Meadow

Every decision with alternatives and rationale; deviations from the plan included.

| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seeded population from a Thomas/Poisson/gradient process (one documented seed, `MEADOW_SEED = 20260535`) | Hand-placed plants; procedural-only with random seed per session | A known truth makes the reveal possible; a fixed seed makes unit tests, the reveal and re-sampling reproducible. Chosen from a scan of consecutive seeds so realized totals sit near the parameter expectations (documented in `design/ecology-model.md`). |
| 2 | Convenience design modeled as "60 % near visible showy clusters + 40 % near the gate" | Free learner placement only; pre-scripted biased set | Free placement IS available (map click or x/z inputs), but the modeled bias reproduces real convenience behaviour even when a learner places "randomly feeling" points; both paths appear in the reveal. Honest description in the design picker. |
| 3 | Wind reduced to ~12 % (not 0) inside the counting view; 0 under reduced motion | Full wind always; hard pause always | 12 % keeps the meadow alive without hurting legibility of the counted plants (counting affordance, like parting the sward); reduced-motion users get a full pause. Documented in `design/realism-matrix.md`. |
| 4 | No bloom in the post pipeline even though the preset table lists `bloom-light` | Bloom on High+ | Nothing in an overcast daylight meadow physically motivates bloom; restrained post (SMAA on Medium+, N8AO on High+ only). The preset array is template heritage; `post.js` reads only `smaa`/`aoEnabled`. |
| 5 | Never thin instances inside the active quadrat (preset density applies outside it) | Thin everywhere for perf | On Low (40 % density) the learner would count a systematically wrong sample — the estimate must match the truth on every preset, or the reveal lies. Perf impact is bounded (one 2 m radius per active quadrat). |
| 6 | Field marks slightly exaggerated in the models (blade widths ~2–3× real; head contrast boosted) | Strict real-world dimensions | At the counting view's pixel scale, real 2–4 mm blades are sub-pixel. Plan §13 explicitly allows exaggeration documented in the realism matrix; the field guide states real dimensions while the in-app note says marks are exaggerated for legibility. |

## Deviations from the plan

- **No `v1.0.0` tag** (plan §8 phase 8): per orchestrator instruction the owner/orchestrator tags releases; package.json is at 1.0.0 and the release workflow is in place, so tagging later fires the same pipeline.
- **`scripts/build-species-models` / `bake-imposters` as offline build steps** (plan §7 tree): species models are built at runtime from `src/systems/species-models.js` and imposters are baked at startup — no offline artifacts to keep in sync. Rationale: models are ~100 lines of primitives each; a build step added nothing.
- **The "reveal vs estimate" table also includes the Bunchgrass cover comparison** and personalized error-source notes (bias highlighted for the convenience design; mis-ID noted when the ID quiz recorded mistakes) — an extension of plan §5.2's reveal to make the error sources concrete for the learner's own run.
- **Systematic design draws a random grid offset once** and the stratified design puts the remainder quadrat in the wet stratum — matching `content/designs.json` copy, which is the learner-facing contract.
