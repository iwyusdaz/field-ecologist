# Learning objectives

Measurable objectives for Field Ecologist: Sampling the Meadow. Each maps to a
standard (NGSS HS-LS2-2 verified in `research/sources.md` #1; Science Practices
3–5: planning investigations, analyzing data, using mathematics).

## LO1 — Design
**Explain why a sampling design is needed and choose one that fits a research
question and a known environmental gradient.**
- Verb: explain / choose. Evidence: hypothesis task (M1), design choice with
  justification (M3, M5 report §design), pre-check items 1, 3, 5.

## LO2 — Quadrat method
**Apply quadrat methods: place quadrats according to a chosen design, identify
species using field marks (shape and size first, colour second), and record
counts and percent-cover bands.**
- Verb: apply. Evidence: fieldwork game (M3): placement per design, top-down
  counting view, field-guide ID workflow, data sheet; ID quiz (M2).

## LO3 — Estimate and quantify uncertainty
**Estimate population density (and cover) from sample data and quantify the
uncertainty of the estimate.**
- Verb: estimate / quantify. Evidence: density computed from the learner's data
  sheet; uncertainty from the re-sampling simulator (SD, 95% percentile
  interval); chart item 1; report §uncertainty.

## LO4 — Diagnose bias and variance
**Explain how design choices — biased selection, too few quadrats, ignoring
strata — affect the accuracy and precision of an estimate.**
- Verb: explain. Evidence: reveal table (observed vs true per species with error
  decomposition), simulator comparisons across designs and n, chart items 2–3,
  pre-check item 3.

## LO5 — Transfer
**Transfer the sampling reasoning to a non-ecological research problem (a school
survey about study habits).**
- Verb: transfer/apply. Evidence: 4 transfer items (M5); report §limitations
  invites the parallel.

## Out of scope (documented)
Real regional flora identification keys, seasonal change, animal (mark–recapture)
sampling. The species are a constructed teaching model — labeled as such
in-app (`content/species.json` meta.statement).
