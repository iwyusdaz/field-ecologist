# Personas

| Persona | Context | Need | How the design meets it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Noor, 16** — Grade 11 biology student | Confident with averages; believes "more data always fixes bias" | To see bias beat sample size | The re-sampling simulator: with the convenience design, raising n from 5 to 40 shifts nothing — the distribution stays off-centre (chart item 2 forces the reading) |
| **Sam, 19** — First-year undergrad, research methods | Can compute a mean; cannot say what sampling error is | To connect the formula to a picture | The histogram + data table + summary numbers (mean, SD, 95% percentile interval) sit beside the estimate they themselves produced |
| **Mr. Ibsen, 40s** — Secondary science teacher | Needs a pre-lab that fits one 45–60 min period and links to the real field trip | Predictable lesson shape, printable data, curriculum mapping | Module structure maps 1:1 to a period; CSV export for class data; teacher guide with timing; NGSS HS-LS2-2 alignment matrix |
| **Dr. Adeyemi** — Research-methods instructor (higher ed) | Wants a bridge from ecological quadrats to survey sampling | An explicit transfer task | Module 5 transfer items: the same four designs applied to surveying students, with the library sample as the convenience case |
| **Text-mode learner** (screen-reader or low-bandwidth user) | Cannot or prefers not to use the 3D view | Full parity | Text mode: the meadow as a 20×20 grid map with moisture zones; sampling a cell reveals structured plant descriptions; same data sheet, reveal, simulator and items |
